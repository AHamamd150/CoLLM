# Physics Process: p p > W Z, W > l nu, Z > l+ l-
# ================================================

[SELECTION_CUTS]
- Select electrons with pT > 15 GeV and |eta| < 2.5
- Select muons with pT > 10 GeV and |eta| < 2.4
- Require exactly 3 leptons
- Require at least one opposite-sign same-flavor lepton pair
- Select invariant mass of the OSSF pair between 76 and 106 GeV (Z candidate)
- Require MET > 30 GeV
- The third lepton (W candidate lepton) must have pT > 20 GeV
- Select transverse mass of W lepton + MET > 30 GeV

[PLOTS_FOR_VALIDATION]
- Plot the following as histograms:
1 Plot the invariant mass of the Z candidate (OSSF pair) in range 60 to 120 GeV
2 Plot the transverse mass of the W candidate (third lepton + MET)
3 Plot the MET distribution
4 Plot the pT of the three-lepton system
5 Plot the pT of the Z candidate
6 Plot the pT of the W candidate lepton
7 Plot delta phi between the Z candidate and MET
8 Plot the invariant mass of all three leptons
9 Plot delta R between the two leptons forming the Z
10 Plot eta of the W candidate lepton

[OUTPUT_STRUCTURE]
- Print event yields after all selection
- save the plots in png format with dpi = 150
