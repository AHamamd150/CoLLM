# Physics Process: p p > H > Z Z, Z > l+ l-, Z > l+ l-
# ================================================================

[SELECTION_CUTS]
- Select electrons with pT > 7 GeV and |eta| < 2.47
- Select muons with pT > 5 GeV and |eta| < 2.7
- Require exactly 4 leptons (electrons or muons)
- Require at least one opposite-sign same-flavor lepton pair with invariant mass between 50 and 106 GeV (Z1 candidate)
- Require a second opposite-sign lepton pair with invariant mass between 12 and 115 GeV (Z2 candidate)
- Select four-lepton invariant mass between 115 and 135 GeV

[PLOTS_FOR_VALIDATION]
- Plot the following as histograms:
1 Plot the four-lepton invariant mass (m_4l) in the range 100 to 150 GeV
2 Plot the invariant mass of Z1 candidate (leading Z) in the range 50 to 110 GeV
3 Plot the invariant mass of Z2 candidate (subleading Z) in the range 10 to 120 GeV
4 Plot the pT of the leading lepton
5 Plot the pT of the four-lepton system
6 Plot delta R between the two leading leptons
7 Plot the eta distribution of all selected leptons
8 Plot the phi distribution of all selected leptons

- Each histogram should have 40 bins.
- Use LaTeX notation for axis labels where applicable.
- Normalize all histograms to unity.

[OUTPUT_STRUCTURE]
- Print cutflow showing number of events after each selection
- Save the following in a CSV file for MLP analysis:
  1- Four-lepton invariant mass
  2- Z1 candidate mass
  3- Z2 candidate mass
  4- pT of the leading lepton
  5- delta R between the two Z candidates
